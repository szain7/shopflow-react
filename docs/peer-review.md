# Partner review preparation

Both reviews are pending. Fill the evidence fields using real interactions.

## D5-5 Architecture review

- [ ] Share [architecture](ARCHITECTURE.md), [orders proposal](orders.md) and one design question with your partner.
- [ ] Ask them to identify two strengths, two risks, and one recommendation. Ask for the file or diagram involved and why each point matters.
- [ ] Record the partner, date, review link or notes location, and their actual feedback.
- [ ] Record your response and whether you accepted or deferred the recommendation.

Evidence to supply: partner, review reference, strengths 1–2, risks 1–2, recommendation and your response.

## D5-6 PR review

- [ ] Review a real partner PR and leave at least five useful comments across correctness, readability, and accessibility, plus one specific praise.
- [ ] For each finding, give the location, observed behavior, impact, and a concrete suggestion or question. Use the [React checklist](ai/react-review.md) for review prompts.
- [ ] Respond to partner comments on your own PR and link any fixes.
- [ ] Save links to the PR, the five comments, the praise, and your responses.

Evidence to supply: partner PR link, comment and praise links, own PR link, and response links.
